SOFTWARE & TECHNOLOGY FOR CONSERVATION

More time for the work that matters.

I help land trusts, conservation organizations, and outdoor nonprofits spend less time wrestling with technology and more time doing their work.

That might mean improving an existing process, connecting systems, making information easier to manage, or building a small tool that fills a gap.

Where I can help.

Volunteer and stewardship coordination

Track volunteer contributions, coordinate activities, and keep records of work completed across properties and projects.

Records and reporting

Organize project information, simplify recurring reports, and make it easier to show funders and supporters what's getting done.

Better-connected systems

Reduce duplicate entry, connect existing tools, and improve workflows that have grown cumbersome over time.

Small, purpose-built applications

When existing tools don't quite fit, build something straightforward that does the job without creating unnecessary complexity.

HOW I WORK

Start with the problem, not the software.

I'm not interested in replacing a system that's already working. I'd rather understand what your organization needs, find the simplest reasonable approach, and build only when it makes sense.

LET'S TALK

What's taking more time than it should?

If your organization has a process that's harder than it needs to be, I'd be glad to hear about it.

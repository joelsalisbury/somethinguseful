SOFTWARE FOR CONSERVATION ORGANIZATIONS

Good work deserves good tools.

Land trusts, conservation groups, and outdoor nonprofits manage a remarkable amount of work with limited time and resources. Property records, volunteer projects, habitat improvements, grants, reporting, and the everyday business of keeping an organization running.

I build software and improve existing systems to make some of that work easier.

01 / THE WORK

A lot happens behind the scenes.

The work of protecting and managing land involves more than what happens in the field. There's information to collect, people to coordinate, projects to track, and a steady stream of obligations to keep up with.

01

Land & stewardship

Properties, monitoring, maintenance, landowner records

02

People & participation

Volunteers, chapters, events, training

03

Projects & equipment

Habitat work, shared tools, schedules, field activities

04

Data & accountability

Grants, outcomes, reporting, organizational records

02 / POSSIBILITIES

Some things worth making easier.

These are examples of the kinds of problems I enjoy working on. Every organization has its own needs, and the right solution depends on what's already in place.

01

Stewardship records

Keep monitoring visits, property notes, photos, and follow-up work organized.

02

Volunteer coordination

Make it easier to plan workdays, record contributions, and understand participation.

03

Habitat projects

Track project milestones, contractors, landowner communications, and outcomes.

04

Equipment sharing

Manage availability, reservations, and maintenance for shared tools and gear.

05

Grant reporting

Bring project information together and reduce repetitive reporting work.

06

Connected systems

Move information between existing tools without entering the same data repeatedly.

03 / THE APPROACH

Small improvements can go a long way.

Sometimes a spreadsheet needs a better structure. Sometimes two systems need to share information. Sometimes a simple web application can replace a process that's become difficult to manage.

I work with organizations to understand what's happening, figure out what would help, and put a practical solution in place. The goal is something people can use and maintain comfortably.

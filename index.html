A particular interest in conservation.

Land trusts, habitat organizations, trail groups, and other conservation nonprofits have important work to do, often with limited time and resources.

I can help with the technology behind that work, whether it's coordinating volunteers, organizing records, improving reporting, or connecting systems that don't work well together.

Sometimes that means building something new. Often it means making better use of what's already there.
